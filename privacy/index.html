Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. This policy is issued by Mathias Storm, mathias@skartved.me, Europe (Denmark), for the Budget Keep Google Sheets add-on.

What Budget Keep is

Budget Keep is a Google Sheets editor add-on. It helps you keep a personal budget in a spreadsheet you control.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your budget, transactions, categories, or bank-statement CSVs. That information stays in your Google spreadsheet, in your Google account.

Budget Keep is built so that it:

  • can only access the spreadsheet you use it in (spreadsheets.currentonly), not your other Drive files or spreadsheets
  • can show a sidebar in Sheets (script.container.ui)
  • does not call external servers
  • does not read your email address
  • does not connect to your bank

If you enter an optional license key, it is stored only in that spreadsheet’s document properties, on Google’s side of your file. We do not receive a copy.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels in the add-on or on this website.

Google account data

When you install Budget Keep, Google shows a consent screen for the two permissions above. Google, not we, handles your Google account sign-in. We do not receive a feed of users from Google.

Payments

If you buy the optional paid upgrade, payment is handled by a payment provider we will name here before any charge is taken (planned: Paddle or Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record). That provider processes the payment details needed for the purchase (for example card or account data, billing email, and VAT information) under their own privacy policy. We ask you to paste a license key into the add-on; we do not ask you to type card numbers into Budget Keep.

Until a payment provider is connected, no paid purchase is possible and no payment data is processed.

Legal bases (GDPR)

For the add-on itself, we do not process your personal data on our systems. Your use of Google Sheets is a relationship with Google. If, later, a support inbox or payment provider processes your email or billing identity, the legal bases will be: taking steps to answer your request or to perform a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and, where required, our legitimate interest in running a small software product fairly (Art. 6(1)(f)), or a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)), for example tax records held by the payment provider.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your budget data. We have no server that holds it. A payment provider, once appointed, will process purchase data as an independent controller or as described in their terms. Google processes the spreadsheet you store in your Google account under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

Retention

We keep no copy of your spreadsheet data. A license key remains in a spreadsheet until you delete it or the spreadsheet. Payment records, once sales begin, will be retained by the payment provider as required for tax and accounting.

Your rights

If we ever hold personal data about you (for example a support email you sent), you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, and data portability, and you may complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or your local EU/EEA supervisory authority. Contact mathias@skartved.me.

Because your budget lives in your own Google Sheet, you can already access, edit, or delete it there, or uninstall the add-on, without asking us.

Children

Budget Keep is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and we will change the date above.
