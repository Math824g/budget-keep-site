A personal budget in the Google Sheet you already have

Budget Keep is a Google Sheets add-on. Set categories, import a bank CSV, and close each month. Your numbers stay in the spreadsheet. There is no bank login, and the add-on cannot see your other files.

How to use it Privacy policy

What it does

  • Set up a simple budget Creates a template in the current spreadsheet — categories, transactions, and a monthly summary — and lets you set amounts in the sidebar.
  • Import a statement Paste a CSV, or read another tab in the same spreadsheet. Budget Keep does not connect to your bank and does not need access to Google Drive.
  • Close the month See budget versus actual. Use any currency, including euro and kroner, and EU date and number formats.
  • Optional paid upgrade €19 per year, when checkout is offered, unlocks saved bank mappings, auto-categorisation rules you can edit, subscription detection, and a yearly report.

Your data stays in the sheet

Budget Keep runs only on the spreadsheet you use it in. It does not collect, store, or send your transactions, and it does not call external servers. The only permissions it asks for are access to this spreadsheet and the ability to show a sidebar.

Please read the privacy policy for the full details, including payments and your rights. The terms of use describe the free features, the optional upgrade, and how a license key works.

How to find it after you install it

  1. Open the Google Sheet you want to use.
  2. In the menu choose Extensions → Budget Keep → Open sidebar.
  3. Click Set up budget sheets.
