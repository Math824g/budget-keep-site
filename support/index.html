Support

How to find Budget Keep after you install it, and how to get help. This is a small product; we aim to reply within a few business days (Europe/Copenhagen).

How to find the add-on after you install it

  1. Open the Google Sheet you want to use (the one you installed Budget Keep in, or any sheet if the add-on is installed on your account).
  2. In the menu bar choose Extensions → Budget Keep.
  3. Click Open sidebar, then Set up budget sheets.

If the menu item is missing, reload the spreadsheet. The first install also shows a welcome dialog that repeats these steps.

Common tasks

  • Change currency or EU date formats: sidebar → Setup.
  • Import a bank statement: sidebar → Import. Paste a CSV, or put the statement in another tab of the same spreadsheet and choose that tab. Budget Keep does not log into your bank and does not need access to Google Drive.
  • Close a month: Extensions → Budget Keep → Close month…, or the Month tab in the sidebar. Results go to the Monthly sheet.
  • Paid features: paste your license key under Extensions → Budget Keep → Enter license key…, or in the sidebar’s Paid tab. The key is stored only in that spreadsheet.

Privacy

Your numbers stay in your spreadsheet. See the privacy policy.

Get in touch

Email mathias@skartved.me with a description of what you expected and what happened. Please do not send real bank statements, copies of your budget, or other personal financial files unless we specifically ask and you are comfortable doing so. We usually cannot debug a problem from a screenshot of a formula alone — describe the menu path and any exact error text.
